Acharya Kautilya's Timeless Principles.

cross fifteen foundational books, Acharya Kautilya constructed antiquity’s most sophisticated system of governance, economic strategy, and political realism. Reorganized into a concept-centered framework for modern decision-makers, this definitive volume distills the Arthashastra into an actionable architecture of power, institutional resilience, and executive discipline.
