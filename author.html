Author

The person behind the reconstruction

Veteran Wg Cdr Vijay Dhanraj Chattur

Author, A Doctrinal Reconstruction of the Acharya Kautilya's Arthashastra

Veteran Wg Cdr Vijay Dhanraj Chattur is an engineer, former Indian Air Force officer, entrepreneur and student of Indian heritage. A graduate of COEP with a Master's degree from the Indian Institute of Science, Bengaluru, he served the Indian Air Force with distinction for twenty years before retiring as a Wing Commander.

He subsequently developed several industrial products as an entrepreneur, including a patented innovation, and contributed extensively to entrepreneurship development. He is a past Chairman of an NGO dedicated to the cause.

His enduring interest in India's intellectual and spiritual heritage has led to several published works on Sikh and Jain traditions, including:

  • Sikhish
    • Zafarnama (Shri Vijay Patra)
    • Shri Vijay Patra
    • Bachitra Natak
    • Chandi Di Vaar
    • Jaap Sahib
  • Jainism
    • Leshya - Key to Kaivalya

His work on Sikh literature has been recognised by Sikh scholars and Gurudwaras.

His present work on Acharya Kautilya's Arthashastra continues his effort to explore and present India's enduring intellectual traditions for the contemporary reader.

Other Works

Brief information on the books

Shri Vijay Patra (Zafarnama)

Published in two companion volumes—one in Marathi poetry and the other in Hindi poetry, both featuring English prose translations. The Zafarnama is Guru Gobind Singh's historic letter to Mughal Emperor Aurangzeb, standing as a monumental document of supreme spirituality, unyielding valor, and absolute integrity. It articulates profound moral principles, exposes Mughal duplicity, reflects India's ancient philosophy of warfare, and champions the ethos that righteous work is worship. Originally composed in Persian—a language inaccessible to most contemporary Sikhs—this translation bridges the linguistic divide, allowing the faithful to deeply engage with its profound meaning. Notably, Shri Suhas Joshi has rendered portions of this translated work into Hindustani classical music.

Bachitra Natak

A metered poetic composition in Braj Bhasha that chronicles pivotal events in the life of Guru Gobind Singh, mirrored by an equally metered Marathi translation. It captures the spiritual triumph of divine souls on the worldly stage through sublime poetry. Its central thesis asserts that enlightened individuals must openly and visibly participate in the righteous struggle against wickedness.

Chandi Di Var

Rooted in the Devi Mahatmyam (Durga Saptashati) from the Markandeya Purana. During an era of severe Mughal oppression when the populace felt disempowered and destitute, this work served as a vital psychological catalyst. By invoking familiar mythological heroic ballads, it elevated societal morale, reshaped public consciousness, and galvanized people to take up arms against tyranny and injustice.

Jaap Sahib

A foundational composition by Guru Gobind Singh integral to the daily recitation (Nitnem) mandated by the Sikh Code of Conduct. In this work, the Guru extols the Divine through His manifold actions, presenting a universal conception of God that transcends sectarian boundaries. The Divine is depicted as supreme, compassionate to the downtrodden, a chastiser of the wicked, omnipresent, transparent, and boundlessly generous without expectation of return. Harmonizing diverse religious and cultural traditions, the composition moves across ten distinct meters tailored to its rhythm and emotional resonance, a structure meticulously preserved in the translation.

Lesya — Key to Kaivalya

Lesya is a foundational concept in Jaina philosophy that governs karma bondage, refines personal character in the present, and shapes spiritual evolution for the future. Recognizing its immense power to alter an individual's trajectory, the work is fittingly titled Lesya—Key to Kaivalya. Bridging the inner and outer worlds, Lesya bridges psychology, physiology, body chemistry, and philosophy into a single practical framework. As the primary architect of human personality, it possesses the unique capacity to transform life patterns at their very core.
