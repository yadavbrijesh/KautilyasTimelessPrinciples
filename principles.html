Timeless Principles

Each principle is drawn from a specific passage of the Arthashastra, then set down in plain language — without softening what Kautilya actually argued.

· Author, Veteran Wg Cdr Vijay Dhanraj Chattur

01 Why strong institutions matter more than strong leaders.Leaders inspire change, but institutions preserve it. Read → 02 Why readiness matters more than action.The best decisions are made long before they are announced. Read → 03 Why wise leaders choose dialogue over conflict.The greatest victory is the one achieved without conflict. Read → 04 Internal weakness is more dangerous than external threats.No external threat can destroy a state that has not first weakened itself. Read → 05 Intelligence is the first line of defence.A ruler who sees clearly rarely needs to act in haste. Read → 06 Wise leaders seek wise counsel.The strength of a decision often depends upon the wisdom that shapes it.
